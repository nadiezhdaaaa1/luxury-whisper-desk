## 3. Automatic renewal (auto-renewal disclosure)
Paid subscriptions **automatically renew** at the end of each billing period (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual, as selected) until you cancel. At the point of sale we will clearly and conspicuously disclose: (a) that the subscription continues until cancelled; (b) the recurring amount and billing frequency; (c) the renewal/cancellation terms; and (d) how to cancel. By subscribing, you authorize us (and our payment processors) to charge your payment method the then-current amount at the start of each billing period.

Where required by law (and as a default good practice), we will send a reminder before your first charge following a trial and before certain renewals, including the amount, date, and a link or instructions to cancel.

## 5. Cancellation (easy cancellation / "click-to-cancel")
You can cancel at any time. Cancellation is available through the same medium you used to subscribe and takes no more than two (2) steps: for web subscriptions, in your account under Manage Subscription → Cancel; for in-app subscriptions, through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings. We do not require you to call, chat, or take additional steps beyond those needed to cancel. Cancellation stops future renewals; you retain Pro access until the end of the current paid period unless otherwise required by law.

## 6. Pause
Where offered, you may pause your subscription instead of cancelling. During a pause, you are not charged; billing resumes when the pause ends unless you cancel. Pause terms (duration, availability) are shown at the time you choose them.

Prices are shown at checkout in your local currency where available and are exclusive of taxes unless stated. We may change prices; we will give advance notice as required by law, and changes apply to the next billing period after notice. Applicable taxes may be added based on your location.
