## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

- **Account information:** name, email, password (hashed), and, if enabled, two-factor authentication details.

We process photographs uploaded by users solely for the purpose of identifying items, maintaining portfolios and providing related features of the Service.
- **Usage and analytics data:** pages viewed, features used, events, session recordings/heatmaps, approximate location derived from IP.
- **Device and technical data:** device type, OS, browser, identifiers, and, for the app, push tokens.
- **Marketing/attribution data:** campaign and referral information (e.g., UTM parameters, ad attribution).
- **Payment data:** processed by our payment providers; we receive limited billing details (e.g., plan, status, last four digits) but not full card numbers.
- **Communications:** messages you send us and your email engagement (opens, clicks) where applicable.

- Analyze usage to improve features and performance.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.
