# Privacy Policy

**Last updated / Effective date:** September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KERIVO STUDIO LIMITED, trading as "PriceYou," a company registered in Scotland (Company No. SC889293) ("PriceYou," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the PriceYou website and web application, and any mobile applications we may release (the "Service").

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

- Account information: name, email address, password (stored hashed) and, if enabled, two-factor authentication details.
- Profile and preferences: selected categories, brands, segments, watchlist entries, target prices, digest frequency, quiet hours and notification settings.
- Date of birth, where you choose to provide it, used only to send you a birthday message or offer. This is optional and is not required to use the Service.
- Subscription and access records: your plan, billing period, renewal dates, and records of when you signed in, accessed or used the Service, and interacted with Signals or other notifications. These records are used to operate the Service and to assess refund requests under the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refunds).
- Usage and analytics data: pages viewed, features used, events, session recordings and heatmaps, and approximate location derived from IP address.
- Device and technical data: device type, operating system, browser, identifiers and, in any mobile app, push tokens.
- Marketing and attribution data: campaign and referral information, including UTM parameters and advertising attribution.
- Payment data: processed by our payment providers. We receive limited billing details such as plan, status and the last four digits of your card, and never the full card number.
- Communications: messages you send us, including refund and support requests, and your email engagement such as opens and clicks.
- User content: watchlist entries, notes and, if and when a portfolio feature is released, photographs of items you own, purchase prices, receipts, certificates and provenance documents you choose to upload.

## 2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

- To provide and operate the Service, including generating and delivering Signals, digests and notifications for the brands and categories you select.
- To create and secure your account, including two-factor authentication and fraud prevention.
- To process subscriptions and payments, manage renewals and cancellations, and assess refund requests.
- To send transactional messages, and, with your consent where required, marketing and seasonal messages.
- To send you a birthday message or offer, where you have provided your date of birth.
- To analyse usage in order to improve features and performance.
- To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

## 3. LEGAL BASES (EEA AND UK USERS)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on the following bases: performance of a contract (providing the Service); your consent (for example, certain cookies, marketing, and providing your date of birth); our legitimate interests (securing and improving the Service, and preventing fraud and abuse of our refund process); and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time.

## 4. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary sense of selling personal data for money. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the Service — for payments, hosting and storage, email and notifications, data collection from public sources, and analytics and attribution — under contracts that limit their use of the data to providing services to us.

We may also disclose information to comply with law, enforce our Terms, protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. Some analytics and advertising activities may be considered "sharing" or a "sale" under certain US state privacy laws; see section 9.

## 5. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our [Cookie Policy](/cookies). Where required, we obtain consent through a cookie banner.

## 6. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, then for a reasonable period to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Subscription, payment and access records are retained for the period required by tax, accounting and consumer-protection rules, and to defend chargebacks and refund disputes. You can request deletion under section 9; some data may be retained where legally required.

## 7. SECURITY

We use administrative, technical and organisational safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls and encryption at rest for stored documents where implemented. No system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. BIOMETRIC INFORMATION

The Service is not intended to collect or process biometric identifiers or biometric information. If and when a feature allowing photographs of items is released, we will not use those photographs for facial recognition, identity verification, biometric identification or the creation of biometric templates.

## 9. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

United States, including California (CCPA/CPRA): depending on your state, you may have the right to know and access, delete, correct, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To opt out, use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link or contact privacy@price.you. We honour recognised opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control, where required.

EEA and UK (GDPR): you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. The UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) is the relevant supervisory authority for data protection matters in the United Kingdom.

To exercise rights, contact privacy@price.you. We will verify your request as required by law.

## 10. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

We may process and store information in the United States, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, for transfers of EEA and UK personal data.

## 11. CHILDREN

The Service is not directed to children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact privacy@price.you and we will delete it.

## 12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version with an updated date and, for material changes, provide additional notice and, where required by law, obtain your consent.
