# Cookie Policy

**Last updated / Effective date:** September 3, 2026

## 1. WHAT COOKIES ARE

Cookies and similar technologies, including local storage, software development kits and pixels, are small files or identifiers stored on your device that help a website or app function, remember preferences and understand usage. This Policy explains how PriceYou (operated by KERIVO STUDIO LIMITED, trading as "PriceYou," a company registered in Scotland (Company No. SC889293)) uses them on our website and web application. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

By continuing to use the Service you acknowledge the use of strictly necessary cookies. Where required by law, we request your consent before using non-essential cookies.

## 2. CATEGORIES WE USE

- Strictly necessary — required for the Service to work, including authentication and session management, security, load balancing and payment flows. These cannot be switched off.
- Functional — remember your preferences and settings.
- Analytics and performance — help us understand usage and improve the Service.
- Marketing and attribution — measure campaigns and attribute sign-ups.

## 3. TOOLS WE USE

Supabase (strictly necessary) — keeps you signed in and secures your session.

Stripe (strictly necessary) — processes payments and prevents fraud.

Google Analytics 4 (analytics) — measures traffic, conversions and feature usage.

Amplitude (analytics) — product analytics: funnels, retention and events.

Microsoft Clarity (analytics) — session recordings, heatmaps and usability analytics.

AppsFlyer (marketing and attribution) — attributes campaigns and measures sign-ups.

Advertising platforms, including Meta and Google (marketing and attribution) — campaign measurement and, where enabled, advertising.

The specific cookies and technologies we use may change as we update the Service. Our cookie consent tool, where available, reflects the categories currently in use.

## 4. CONSENT

Where required, including for visitors in the EEA and the United Kingdom, we request your consent before setting non-essential cookies through a cookie banner, and you can accept, reject or customise your choices. Strictly necessary cookies are set without consent because the Service cannot function without them. In the United States you can exercise opt-out rights for "sale" and "sharing" and for targeted advertising as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), including through recognised opt-out preference signals.

## 5. MANAGING COOKIES

You can change your consent choices at any time via the cookie settings link in the footer. You can also control cookies through your browser settings; blocking or deleting cookies may affect functionality. Mobile identifiers can be limited through your device operating-system settings.
